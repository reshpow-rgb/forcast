RiseUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

RiseUp is an alarm clock app. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information that stays on your phone

Your alarms, wake-up missions, sound choices, streak history and appearance settings are stored only on your device. We don't have accounts, and we don't receive or store this information on any server. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

If you choose a song from your phone as an alarm sound, the app only reads that one file, only to play it.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as your phone's advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App interactions and diagnostic information (for example, ad views, taps and crash data)

This is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time from "Privacy settings" in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

RiseUp Premium is bought through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google, not by us; the app only checks with Google Play whether Premium has been purchased. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Permissions

  • Alarms & reminders, full-screen notifications, notifications: to ring at the exact time and show the alarm on your lock screen.
  • Vibrate, keep awake, run after restart: to vibrate, keep ringing, and re-set your alarms after your phone restarts.
  • Internet: to load ads and check purchases.

Children

RiseUp is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
